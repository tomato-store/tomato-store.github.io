About the Project

Growing & harvesting, fully automated

Tomato Store builds an end-to-end automated pipeline for plant cultivation and harvesting. From environmental sensing and growth management to fruit detection and robotic picking, every stage is coordinated by a Master AI and connected over ROS 2.

🌱

Smart Cultivation

ESP32-based smart-farm sensors monitor the growing environment in real time.

🤖

Robotic Harvesting

Dual-arm manipulators and mobile robots detect and harvest ripe fruit.

🧠

Master AI Control

A Master AI orchestrates every device, with ROS 2 handling all communication.

System Architecture

How the pieces connect

A Master AI sits at the top of the hierarchy, ROS 2 acts as the communication backbone, and a fleet of robots and sensor nodes carries out the work.

🧠 Master AI Orchestration & decision-making
🔗 ROS 2 Communication backbone for all nodes
🦾 Dual-arm SO-ARM101 Precision harvesting
🚗 TurtleBot3 Waffle Pi Mobile transport
🦿 OpenMANIPULATOR Manipulation tasks
⚡ Jetson AGX Orin / Thor On-board AI compute
🍓 Raspberry Pi Edge device control
🌡️ ESP32 Sensor Hub Smart-farm sensing
