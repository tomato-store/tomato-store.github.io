About the Project

Growing & harvesting, fully automated

Tomato Store builds an end-to-end automated pipeline for plant cultivation and harvesting. From environmental sensing and growth management to fruit detection and robotic picking, every stage is coordinated by a Master AI and connected over ROS 2.

🌱

Smart Cultivation

ESP32-based smart-farm sensors monitor the growing environment in real time.

🤖

Robotic Harvesting

Dual-arm manipulators and mobile robots detect and harvest ripe fruit.

🧠

Master AI Control

A Master AI orchestrates every device, with ROS 2 handling all communication.
